Detailed Schema Definitions

1. Users Collection (users)

Field NameBSON TypeKey / IndexDescription
_idObjectIdPRIMARY KEYUnique surrogate identifier for user account.
nameString—Full legal name of the user (max 80 characters).
emailStringUNIQUE INDEXLowercase email address used for login authentication.
passwordStringselect: falseBcrypt cryptographic hash (salt rounds factor 10).
roleStringEnumRole token: donor or admin.
phoneString—Contact mobile number for emergency notifications.
isBlockedBoolean—Administrative moderation flag (default: false).

2. Donors Collection (donors)

Field NameBSON TypeKey / IndexDescription
_idObjectIdPRIMARY KEYUnique donor record surrogate identifier.
userObjectIdFOREIGN KEYReferences users._id (strictly unique per donor).
bloodGroupStringCOMPOUND INDEXBlood group (A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-).
cityStringCOMPOUND INDEXMunicipality / City indexed together with blood group.
ageNumberMin 18, Max 65Compliant with National Blood Transfusion Council regulations.
availableBooleanDefault: trueLive readiness switch toggled by the voluntary donor.
statusStringEnumModeration status: pending, approved, or rejected.

3. Organ Pledges Collection (organpledges)

Field NameBSON TypeKey / IndexDescription
_idObjectIdPRIMARY KEYUnique surrogate key for pledge audit record.
pledgeIdStringUNIQUE INDEXNational tracking format identifier (e.g. NOTTO-LL-84920).
fullNameString—Full name printed on digital Form 7 Donor Card.
emailStringIndexedCitizen contact email address.
organsPledgedArray<String>—Heart, Kidneys, Liver, Lungs, Pancreas, Intestines, Corneas, etc.
emergencyContactNameStringTHOTA RequirementName of primary next-of-kin legal custodian.
emergencyContactPhoneStringTHOTA RequirementMobile number of legal next-of-kin.
