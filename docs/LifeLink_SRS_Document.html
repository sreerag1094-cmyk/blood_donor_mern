1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose & Scope

This Software Requirements Specification defines the technical and operational blueprint for the LifeLink HealthTech platform. The system bridges acute blood shortages and empowers citizens across India to register as voluntary blood donors, explore organ viability golden hours, pre-screen medical fitness via AI, and pledge organs directly with NOTTO under Indian law (THOTA 1994).

Statutory Framework: Connected with the National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organization (NOTTO) under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Govt. of India. Emergency 24x7 Helpline: 1800-11-4770.

2. Functional Modules

Module 1: Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

  • User sign-up and login with salted bcrypt hashing (work factor 10).
  • Cryptographic stateless JSON Web Token (JWT) issuance (10-day lifespan).
  • Role separation between voluntary donors and system administrators.

Module 2: Blood Donor Directory & Geographic Matching

  • Donor profiles recording blood group (A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-), city, and state.
  • Search queries with compound indexing { bloodGroup: 1, city: 1 } for sub-50ms response.
  • Live readiness toggle allowing donors to declare current availability.

Module 3: NOTTO Organ Donation Module & Digital Form 7 Card

  • Multi-organ picker: Heart, Kidneys, Liver, Lungs, Pancreas, Intestines, Corneas, and Tissues.
  • Captures legal next-of-kin information as required by the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act (THOTA).
  • Generates instant printable Form 7 National Organ Donor Cards with tracking IDs formatted as NOTTO-LL-XXXXX.
  • Deep-link referral bridge redirecting citizens to the official MoHFW NOTTO portal.

Module 4: Medical Golden Hours Video Presentation

  • 7-scene interactive video showcasing ischemic preservation windows: Heart (4-6h), Liver (8-15h), Kidneys (24-36h), Corneas (< 6h retrieval, stored 14 days), Platelets (5 days).
  • Interactive controls (Play, Pause, Scrubber, Fullscreen) and Web Speech API audio voice narration.

Module 5: AI Donor Eligibility Pre-Screening Engine

  • Assesses Age (18-65), Weight (≥ 45kg), NBTC 6-month tattoo interval, 24h alcohol restriction, acute antibiotic therapy, and recovery intervals (90d M / 120d F).
  • Delivers an instant 0–100% readiness score with personalized clinical advice.
  • Clarifies that blood deferrals do not affect organ donation pledge eligibility.

Module 6: 24/7 LifeLink AI Health Assistant (Chatbot)

  • Persistent floating widget answering clinical queries with SpeechSynthesis readout.
  • Built-in knowledge engine and offline resilience for uninterrupted emergency guidance.
